# STUDY EXPERIMENT ON AI APPLICATIONS: AI IN ROBOTICS

## Aim
To study the applications of Artificial Intelligence in robotics and understand how AI helps robots perform intelligent tasks.

## Objectives
1. To understand the relationship between AI and robotics.
2. To study the components of an intelligent robot.
3. To understand AI techniques used in robotics.
4. To identify the applications, advantages, and limitations of AI in robotics.

## Theory
Robotics is a field concerned with designing, building, and operating robots. Artificial Intelligence enables robots to interpret sensor information, recognise objects, plan actions, make decisions, and respond to changing environments.

A robot may follow fixed instructions, while an AI-enabled robot can use AI techniques to interpret information and make decisions for certain tasks.

## Components of an AI Robot

### 1. Sensors
Sensors collect information from the environment. Examples include cameras, distance sensors, and touch sensors.

### 2. Perception System
The perception system interprets sensor information and helps the robot recognise objects and surroundings.

### 3. Knowledge Representation
It stores useful information about objects, locations, and the environment.

### 4. Planning and Decision-Making
This component determines the actions required to achieve a particular goal.

### 5. Control System
The control system converts decisions into commands for the robot's movements.

### 6. Actuators
Actuators such as motors and robotic joints perform physical actions.

### 7. Learning Module
A learning module can help improve the robot's performance using data or feedback.

## AI Techniques Used in Robotics

1. Computer Vision: enables robots to recognise objects and interpret images.
2. Machine Learning: helps robots learn patterns from data.
3. Path Planning: helps robots find suitable routes while avoiding obstacles.
4. Natural Language Processing: allows some robots to understand human commands.
5. Reinforcement Learning: enables an agent to learn actions through rewards and feedback.
6. Localisation and Mapping: helps robots estimate their position and create maps of their surroundings.

## Working of an AI Robot
1. The robot receives a task or goal.
2. Sensors collect information about the surroundings.
3. The perception system interprets the collected information.
4. The AI system identifies objects and relevant environmental conditions.
5. The planning module selects a suitable action.
6. The control system sends commands to actuators.
7. The robot receives new sensor feedback and adjusts its actions when required.

## Applications of AI in Robotics

### 1. Industrial Robotics
Robots help with assembly, product inspection, sorting, and manufacturing.

### 2. Healthcare
Robots can assist with rehabilitation, hospital supply delivery, and selected medical procedures under professional supervision.

### 3. Agriculture
AI-powered robots can monitor crops, identify weeds, and support precision farming.

### 4. Warehousing
Robots can transport goods, sort parcels, and assist with inventory management.

### 5. Domestic Robotics
Robotic vacuum cleaners and other home-assistance devices can automate selected household tasks.

### 6. Space Exploration
Robots help explore environments where direct human access is difficult.

### 7. Search and Rescue
Robots can help survey hazardous areas and provide information to emergency response teams.

## Advantages
- Performs repetitive tasks consistently.
- Can work in certain hazardous environments.
- Improves productivity in suitable applications.
- Can respond to environmental information using sensors and AI.
- Supports precision and automation.

## Limitations and Challenges
- Robots can be expensive to develop and maintain.
- Sensor errors can affect performance.
- Unpredictable environments create challenges.
- Safety testing and human supervision are important.
- Cybersecurity and privacy must be considered.

## Result
The study of AI applications in robotics was completed. The components, working, AI techniques, applications, advantages, and limitations of intelligent robotic systems were understood.
